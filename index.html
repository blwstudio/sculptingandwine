03 Why clay

Clay is the antithesis of a digital world.

We spend our days pinged, scrolled and notified. Always reachable, rarely present, living in our heads and our devices, moving at speed, rarely stopping long enough to feel our own hands.

Working with clay asks the opposite of us. To slow down. To be present. There is no screen, no notifications, no rush. Just you, your hands, and something being made from nothing.

These evenings are not about perfect technique or finished products. They are about the quiet, restorative act of making, with a glass of wine and good company alongside.

04 Is this for me?

For women and mothers who need an evening to call their own.

This is for you if…

  • You would love a creative night out that is not just another dinner or drinks.
  • You want some time for yourself, to slow down and switch off the screen.
  • You are a total beginner and want a relaxed, no-judgement space to try clay.
  • You are looking for a lovely thing to do with friends, or a way to meet new people.

You do not need…

  • Any previous experience, none at all.
  • To bring anything. All materials and guidance are provided.
  • To be "arty". If you can hold a glass of wine, you can do this.
  • To worry about mess. That is half the fun.

We design these evenings for mothers and women, but all genders are very welcome to our evening class.

07 Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need any experience?

None at all. These evenings are designed for total beginners. I will guide you through every step, and there is no pressure to make anything "perfect". It is about the process, not the result.

How do I book?

All tickets are booked through Ticket Tailor, our secure booking partner. Just tap any "Book your ticket" button and it will take you straight there. When you book, we will add you to our mailing list so we can send you everything you need to know about the evening. You can unsubscribe at any time.

What is included in the price?

All your clay, tools and full guidance, plus a glass of wine to sip while you make. You just need to turn up.

What will I make?

On 22 October you will hand-build your own sculptural pumpkin, inspired by the bold, joyful, dotted forms of Yayoi Kusama. I will show you the techniques and you will make it your own.

What is the Christmas Special?

On Thursday 19 November we are running a festive evening that pairs clay with a calligraphy element, so you can make a hand-built piece for the tree as well as name cards for your table. Email hello@blwceramics.studio to be added to the mailing list.

Do I take my piece home the same night?

Yes. We use air-dry clay, so there is no kiln and no waiting. You will leave with your piece in hand the same evening.

Can I come with friends?

Absolutely, it is a lovely night out for a group. Just book your places together. Looking to book the whole evening privately? Get in touch.

What if I cannot make it?

Drop me a line as early as you can and I will do my best to help.
