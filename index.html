05 Why clay & calligraphy

The antithesis of a digital world.

We spend our days pinged, scrolled and notified. Always reachable, rarely present, rarely stopping long enough to feel our own hands.

Clay and calligraphy ask the opposite of us. To slow down. To be present. Both are cool, tactile and entirely analogue, the gentle rhythm of shaping and of a nib moving across paper.

These evenings are not about perfect technique. They are about the quiet, restorative act of making, with a glass of wine and good company alongside.

08 Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need any experience?

None at all, in clay or calligraphy. These evenings are designed for total beginners. I will guide you through every step, and there is no pressure to make anything "perfect". It is about the process, not the result.

What will I make?

Two things to take home: a hand-built clay decoration for your tree, and your own place cards lettered by hand in calligraphy for the festive table.

How do I book?

Tickets are booked through Ticket Tailor, our secure booking partner. Just tap any "Book your ticket" button and it will take you straight there. When you book, we will add you to our mailing list so we can send you everything you need to know. You can unsubscribe at any time.

What is included in the price?

All your clay, tools, calligraphy materials and full guidance, plus mulled wine and mince pies. Everything you make goes home with you the same night. You just need to turn up.

Do I take my pieces home the same night?

Yes. We use air-dry clay, so there is no kiln and no waiting. You will leave with your decoration and your cards the same evening.

What is the Muse event?

Separately from our own evening, Beth is guiding the clay at "A Handmade Christmas", a festive ornament-making afternoon hosted by Muse on Saturday 28 November, 3 to 5pm at Hello Josephine (£45pp). It is booked through Muse, so please use the "Find out more" link for their details.

Can I come with friends?

Absolutely, it is a lovely night out for a group. Just book your places together. Looking to book the whole evening privately? Get in touch.
