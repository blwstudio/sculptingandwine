02 Why clay

Clay is the antithesis of a digital world.

We spend our days pinged, scrolled and notified. Always reachable, rarely present, living in our heads and our devices, moving at speed, rarely stopping long enough to feel our own hands.

Working with clay asks the opposite of us. To slow down. To be present. There's no screen, no notifications, no rush. Just you, your hands, and something being made from nothing.

These workshops aren't about perfect technique or finished products. They're about the quiet, restorative act of making, with a glass of wine and good company alongside.

03 Is this for me?

For women and mothers who need an evening to call their own.

This is for you if…

  • You'd love a creative night out that isn't just another dinner or drinks.
  • You want some time for yourself, to slow down and switch off the screen.
  • You're a total beginner and want a relaxed, no-judgement space to try clay.
  • You're looking for a lovely thing to do with friends, or a way to meet new people.

You don't need…

  • Any previous experience, none at all.
  • To bring anything. All materials and guidance are provided.
  • To be "arty." If you can hold a glass of wine, you can do this.
  • To worry about mess. That's half the fun.

We're designing these workshops for mothers and women, but all genders are very welcome to our evening class.

06 Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need any experience?

None at all. These evenings are designed for total beginners. I'll guide you through every step, and there's no pressure to make anything "perfect". It's about the process, not the result.

How do I book?

All tickets are booked through Ticket Tailor, our secure booking partner. Just tap any "Book your ticket" button and it'll take you straight there. When you book, we'll add you to our mailing list so we can send you everything you need to know about the evening. You can unsubscribe at any time.

What's included in the price?

All your clay, tools and full guidance, plus a glass of wine to sip while you make. You just need to turn up.

What will I make?

You'll hand-build your own piece to take home, think a small sculptural pot or trinket dish inspired by the bold, joyful forms of Niki de Saint Phalle. I'll show you the techniques and you'll make it your own.

What's the optional paint session?

Once your piece has dried, you can come back the following week (Thursday 17 September) for a relaxed 1.5-hour evening (£31) to paint it in your own colours. Book both evenings together for £75 and save £5. It's completely optional: add it when you book, or decide on the night.

Do I take my piece home the same night?

Yes. We use air-dry clay, so there's no kiln and no waiting. You'll leave part one with your piece in hand. If you've added the paint session, simply let it dry fully and bring it back to paint the following week.

Can I come with friends?

Absolutely, it's a brilliant night out for a group. Just book your places together. Looking to book the whole evening privately? Get in touch.

What if I can't make it?

Drop me a line as early as you can and I'll do my best to help.
